About me

My name is Ekaterina Tumanova. I hold three higher education degrees. In 2015, I graduated from Ulyanovsk State University in a field related to ecology. In 2018, I completed a degree at the Institute of Economics and Business of Ulyanovsk State University. In 2021, I graduated from the Volga State University of Technology, Institute of Forestry and Nature Management. After graduating, I worked in my field within environmental protection organizations of the Ulyanovsk region.

However, at the beginning of 2026, I decided to make a major career change and move into frontend development. I am now actively learning HTML, CSS/SCSS, JavaScript, Git/GitHub, TypeScript, and React, and working with Vite, REST APIs, and modern development tools. Alongside this, I complete study projects and continue building my portfolio. Some of my projects and coursework are showcased on my portfolio website, which also includes links to my profiles, courses, and certificates.

For me, moving into frontend development is a deliberate decision to master an entirely new profession. My main goal right now is to systematize the knowledge I've gained, deepen it, and gain practical experience developing as part of a team. That is why I decided to join Rolling Scopes School.
